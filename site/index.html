x402 on Incentiv

x402 payments in USDC on Incentiv. Sellers and buyers use the stock @x402 packages and register one Incentiv scheme. The facilitator at https://x402.incentiv.io verifies and settles the payments.

Two schemes

incentiv-eoa-tx

The payer is a plain wallet. It signs a USDC transfer and pays gas in CENT.

incentiv-userop

The payer is an Incentiv smart account. It signs an ERC-4337 user operation and pays gas in USDC through the Unified Token Paymaster, or in CENT from the account.

These are custom schemes, so a buyer needs @incentiv/x402 to pay. The standard exact scheme cannot run on Incentiv today; see why.

How it works

  1. The buyer requests a paid route. The seller answers 402 with a PAYMENT-REQUIRED header.
  2. The buyer signs the exact payment that was quoted and retries with a PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header.
  3. The seller asks the facilitator to /verify and /settle it.
  4. The seller serves the content once /settle reports success: true.

The facilitator holds no private key. It only submits payments that buyers already signed.
