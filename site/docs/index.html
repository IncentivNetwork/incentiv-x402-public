Overview

@incentiv/x402 adds two payment schemes for Incentiv (eip155:24101) to the stock @x402 packages, and includes the facilitator that verifies and settles them.

Roles

RoleDoesUses
Seller (resource server)Answers 402 with a price, and serves the content after settlement@x402/express, @x402/next or @x402/hono, plus IncentivEoaTxScheme and IncentivUserOpScheme
Buyer (client, agent)Signs the quoted payment and retries the request@x402/fetch or @x402/axios, plus IncentivEoaTxClientScheme or IncentivUserOpClientScheme
FacilitatorVerifies the signed payment, submits it to the chain, reports the resulthttps://x402.incentiv.io, or your own

Schemes

SchemePayerPaymentGas
incentiv-eoa-txA plain wallet (EOA)A signed USDC transferPaid by the payer in CENT
incentiv-useropAn Incentiv smart accountA signed ERC-4337 user operationPaid by the payer in USDC through the Unified Token Paymaster, or in CENT from the account
  • A seller can offer one or both. A buyer pays with the first one it supports.
  • An EOA can have one payment settling at a time. A smart account can have many in flight, because each payment uses its own nonce lane.

Costs

The facilitator charges nothing. The buyer pays gas on top of the price. On 2026-11-19, a payment cost about 2 CENT of gas from an EOA, or about 0.03 USDC through the paymaster, whose minimum is 0.026 USDC.

Limitations

  • These are custom schemes. Clients without @incentiv/x402 cannot pay, and stock exact clients do not recognise them.
  • Expiry is enforced by the facilitator, not the chain. A signed payment that is never settled stays valid on chain until its nonce is used. A payer can cancel it; see the spec.
  • Incentiv has no testnet. Test with small amounts on mainnet.
